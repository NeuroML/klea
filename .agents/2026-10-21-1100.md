## Errors and Lessons

  the design uses plan history plus the automated-replan budget and HITL.
- Do not skip a guessed `/`; it may be a genuine `run_command` target. Improve
  context and truncation instead.
- Cached tokens mean cache reads (savings); cache-creation writes remain part
  of input tokens and are provider-specific (Anthropic).
- Another agent worked concurrently on docs/agent files; only explicitly
  scoped files were staged and committed.
