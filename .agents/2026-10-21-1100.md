## Errors and Lessons
- Deterministic plan-text comparison cannot distinguish semantic repeats;
  the design uses plan history plus the automated-replan budget and HITL.
- Do not skip a guessed `/`; it may be a genuine `run_command` target. Improve
  context and truncation instead.
- Cached tokens mean cache reads (savings); cache-creation writes remain part
  of input tokens and are provider-specific (Anthropic).
- Another agent worked concurrently on docs/agent files; only explicitly
  scoped files were staged and committed.
- Rejected a never-ending graph/post-answer HITL and a plan-document artifact:
  both add lifecycle/API complexity or special-case only plan output. The
  chosen design keeps per-turn graph runs and adds a bounded evidence record.
- `continue` replaces the goal; a stale goal would anchor the new
  implementation incorrectly.

## Open Questions
- E2E reproduction of repeated plan failures remains difficult; continue
  testing the history/guidance path in real runs.
- Cross-run "plan, then implement later" should be exercised manually with a
  full graph run.
- Cache-creation writes are intentionally not surfaced in the UI.
