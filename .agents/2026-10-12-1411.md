## Errors and Lessons

- Inline LSP diagnostics were stale after cross-file renames in this
  environment; trust `ty`/`ruff`/`pytest` over them.
- Ruff isort uses `combine-as-imports = false`: `import x as y` must be its own
  statement, so "collapse into one block" is impossible without a repo-wide
  isort config change.
- docutils "Block quote ends without a blank line": a `Label:` with no blank
  line followed by a *wrapped* bullet triggers it; wrapped `Args:` field
  bodies also warn.  Keep tool-docstring bullets/args single-line.
- A full `pytest` run rewrites the tracked `vector_stores/nml-elife/*`
  fixtures; restore them afterwards (don't commit).
