## Errors and Lessons
- The 24k cap was an arbitrary "safety margin" (its own comment said so); the
  model in the logs has a 1M-token catalog context, so it was about 0.6% of the
  window. Tool implementations already bound their own output.
- The budget loop used `break`: one oversized failed step demoted every later
  failed step, including small ones. Never truncate the evidence needed to judge
  success criteria.
- The remaining `ty` diagnostic at `schemas.py:365` is the pre-existing
  `status` override, not from this work.

## Open Questions
- Batch evaluations: currently every homogeneous runnable batch is evaluated;
  linear `depends_on` chains evaluate one step at a time because the evaluator
  is what resolves the frontier. Should evaluation be deferred over N steps?
  Requires provisional completion or an execution frontier, and flush points
  for failures, review gates and reasoning steps. Deferred until benchmarking
  shows whether the evaluator cost is material.
