## Scope alignment

`klea_eval` (`eval_pkg`) is the Klea-specific evaluation harness: it exists to
evaluate Klea and is not a general-purpose evaluation harness or benchmarking
platform.  Every suite, fixture and check targets Klea's own agent or RAG
server.  This confirms the already-decided direction (the three won't-implement
items closed in 8a75c162: no harness-level sandbox, no general adapter
contract, no repository/container binding) and makes that remit explicit in the
user and developer documentation.
