## Errors and Lessons

- Wrapped bullets directly under `Use when:` / `Do not use for:` cause
  docutils "Unexpected indentation"; and unescaped `*`/`**` in examples are
  read as inline markup.  With `-W` these fail the build.  Fix: blank line
  after the label, and backtick examples.
- `:func:` vs `:class:` on `difflib.SequenceMatcher` would fail `-W` once
  automoduled; intersphinx is configured for the stdlib.
- `ripgrep` only honours `.gitignore` when a `.git` directory is present
  (`require_git` default); outside a repo it searches ignored files.
- `block_anchor`/`context_aware` had an O(n^2) worst case (inner scan to the
  next last-anchor line).  Fixed with precomputed anchor positions + `bisect_left`
  (O(n log n)), preserving "first hit only" semantics; port also had an
  unbounded middle-line loop, fixed to `min(size-1, actual-1)`.
- Fallback matchers substitute `new_string` **verbatim** (opencode semantics);
  they do not re-apply the matched block's indentation.  Exact match from a
  raw read is the reliable path.
- Prefer `difflib.SequenceMatcher.ratio()` over a hand-rolled Levenshtein
  (stdlib, no custom code); the 0.65 thresholds are now metric-dependent
  heuristics.
- Ruff: `ISC004` (implicit string concat in a collection) and `RUF059`
  (unused unpacked vars) bit the new tests.
