## Decisions

### 1. Planner evidence selects, then degrades — it never drops

`KleaAgentState.planner_evidence()` delegates to `render_plan_evidence`.
Selected entries render in full via `StepOutput.render()`: failed steps, the
transitive `depends_on` closure of every pending step, Evaluator-flagged steps
(derived from `step_incomplete` verdicts), the last completed step, and the
latest abandoned `plan_history` revision. Every other recorded step degrades to
`StepOutput.summary()` plus its step reference, so nothing disappears and no
payload is clipped mid-text. The rule lives in
`devdocs/system/agent-planner-evidence-rendering.md` and the method's docstring
defers to it. No `references` field was added to `StepSchema` — the consumption
edge is `depends_on`, read transitively. The spill-pointer half of the fallback
stays designed-but-unimplemented.

### 2. Evaluator observations stay narrower than the flat rendering

`OperationalEvaluator._observations_text` carries only the current batch's steps
plus their transitive `depends_on` closure, rendered by
`evidence.render_step_observations`. The Evaluator deliberately does not see
every recorded observation.

### 3. The flat `observations_text()` became a budgeted delegate

Previously an unbudgeted, step-ordered dump used by the ReasoningNode and the
tools picker. It now delegates to `evidence.render_step_observations` under the
shared `MAX_EVIDENCE_CHARS` budget (deferred import: `evidence.py` imports
`schemas` at module level). Admission order is errored steps first, then the
remaining steps newest-first; the first step whose full render would overflow is
demoted to entry summaries with a neutral omission line, together with every
later step. The answer node keeps its own budget
(`MAX_OBSERVATIONS_CHARS = 48_000`). Docs updated in `agent-node-contracts.md`,
`agent-planner-evidence-rendering.md` and `streams.md`.

Validation: `pytest tests/test_schemas.py tests/test_reasoning.py
tests/test_evidence.py tests/test_planner_evidence.py` → 101 passed;
`ruff check --fix` / `ruff format` clean. `ty check schemas.py` reports one
pre-existing diagnostic (`PlanSchema.status` overriding
`PlannerPlanSchema.status`), unrelated to these changes.
