## Goal

Stop a failed plan step from ending the run while other steps are still
pending: the agent should replan around the failure instead of reporting an
error. Keep the changelog, the generated Mermaid topology and the C4 component
doc in sync, and cover the routing with a graph test.

## Decision

Proposal 2 was chosen over status-aware planner validation.

Status-aware planner validation (rejecting a plan whose status is not done)
was rejected because a plan may legitimately succeed with a failed step, so
validating the plan status in the Planner would wrongly reject valid plans.
Routing the failed-but-pending case back to the Planner (`need_replan`, a
self-edge to planning) keeps the failure local to the step and lets the
Planner re-plan around it.
