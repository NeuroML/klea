## Changes

1. Spinner lifecycle (`ui/tui/app.py`, `ui/tui/components/turn_status.py`):
   `_run_query` stops the turn spinner unconditionally in its `finally`
   through a new `TurnStatus.stop_spinner()`, so a stream that ends with no
   terminal event cannot leave the timer running. `stop_spinner()` tears down
   only the timer and leaves the status label intact, so an error or an
   awaiting-input note is not clobbered.
2. Approval previews (`mcp/path_detect.py`): `_recover_anchor` recovers a
   locatable fragment (the stripped token, then its basename) when the
   normalised token is not a literal substring, and `_preview_details`
   centres its window on that anchor. This stops a detected token being
   hidden by a head truncation.
3. Status panel (`ui/tui/components/status_panel.py`): `_section_signature`
   plus an attribute stamp let `_reconcile_sections` keep an unchanged
   section mounted instead of rebuilding every section on every streamed
   event. The per-tick re-render cost during streaming was the reason for
   the change.
