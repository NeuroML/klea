## Decisions

- No open rubric design question blocked this work.  The pages document the
  implemented CLI surface only; `rubrics` is described as an optional case
  field layered on task success, and a rubric-declaring case with nothing
  machine-checkable keeps its settled outcome (`grade-missing`, exit 4).  The
  machine-checkable `criteria` predicate form and the scale-rubric pass
  threshold stay deferred in `devdocs/backlog.md` and ADR-0061.
- Exit codes are documented as two separate sets because they genuinely
  differ: `run` returns 0/1/2/4 and `check` returns 0/1/2/3.
